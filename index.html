Ning Jia

I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market.

Welcome to my page! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics in the Department of Spatial Economics at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.

My research focuses on housing markets and urban economics, with particular interests in search and matching in the housing market, buyer and seller behavior, and the effects of macroprudential policies on housing-market outcomes. My research primarily uses micro-level data and empirical analyses motivated and disciplined by economic theory.

Supervisors: Jan Rouwendal, Florian Sniekers, Hans R.A. Koster
